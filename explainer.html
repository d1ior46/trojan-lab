⚡ Trojan activated — now what?

A hardware Trojan can do very different things once its trigger fires. Depending on what the attacker built, it might:

  • 🔓
    Leak secrets — quietly send out keys or private data
  • ✏️
    Corrupt results — flip bits so the output is wrong
  • 💥
    Shut it down — freeze or kill the system (denial of service)
  • 🔋
    Drain power — waste energy and wear the device out
  • 🚪
    Open a backdoor — hand the attacker secret control
  • 🐢
    Degrade it — slow it down or make it unreliable
